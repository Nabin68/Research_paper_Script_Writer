## 🔑 THE 6 RULES FOR A HOOK ON THIS PAGE
1. **6th-grade English.** If a 12-year-old (tech OR non-tech) can't understand it instantly,
   rewrite it. No "LLM / embeddings / attention / benchmark" in the hook.
2. **Lead with ONE shocking number or comparison.** "$80 billion", "21× faster", "33% → 72%",
   "1 trillion minutes", "as accurate as a lab test". One number, not five.
3. **Name a giant or an underdog.** Google, OpenAI, Claude, Nvidia, Meta — OR a nameable person
   ("this Indian researcher", "a 22-year-old"). A protagonist to root for or against.
4. **Stay on the paper.** We explain research. The hook must promise the *finding*, not drift
   into a generic story, life-fact, or motivational line. Channel-centric, always.
5. **First 3 seconds = everything.** Put the wow in second one. Never open with "In this video"
   or a slow setup or "Day 5 of 100".
6. **Pick a proven feeling:** shock, "you're being fooled", "nobody's talking about this",
   "the small one beat the big one", or "this affects YOUR brain/body/job/money".

## ✅ WHAT WINS ON THIS PAGE (from real data)
- **Numbers & comparisons as the hook** — the single most reliable device.
  *"Microsoft doubled ChatGPT's accuracy from 33% to 72% with one line."*
- **Named underdog vs. giants** — *"Google and OpenAI are running behind this Indian guy…"* (45.8k)
- **Absurd actor + huge $ stake + secrecy** — *"A toothpaste company killed an $80B industry…"* (60.9k)
- **Brain / mind / body / your-life** — *"AI models turned into psychopaths after Twitter"* (32.8k);
  *"a device that moves your hand without you moving it"* (43.8k)
- **"You're doing it wrong / it's fake"** — *"The 'thinking' you see ChatGPT do is fake."*

## ❌ WHAT KILLS A HOOK HERE (avoid)
- Vague, no number, no name: *"Scientists built a new memory system."* (1.4k views)
- Pure jargon / mechanism: *"A single-layer RL fine-tuning method…"* (1.2k)
- History framed as history: *"In 1950, Turing wrote a paper…"* (1.2k)
- Storytelling/motivational drift that isn't about the finding.
- Long wind-up before the wow.

## 🧩 HOOK PATTERNS TO PLUG THE PAPER INTO
Fill the [brackets] with the paper's facts. Use 8–15 words, one breath.
1. **Number punch:** *"[Company] just made AI [do X] [N× / N%] better — with [tiny cause]."*
2. **Underdog vs giants:** *"[Google/OpenAI] are running behind [named person] — [he] just [big claim]."*
3. **Absurd actor + stake:** *"A [unexpected actor] just [killed/broke] a [$N] industry, and nobody's talking about it."*
4. **It's-fake / you're-fooled:** *"The [thing you trust about AI] is fake — here's what's really happening."*
5. **Your body/brain/job:** *"[Company]'s new AI can [read your body / your mind / take your job] — and it's already here."*
6. **Small beat big:** *"A [tiny/cheap/free] AI just beat [expensive/giant] one at [task]."*

## 🖐️ HOOK + RE-HOOK (the retention move)
Say the hook, then within ~3–5 seconds hit a **re-hook** — a second line that re-opens the
loop so nobody scrolls: *"And the reason why is even crazier."* / *"But that's not the shocking
part."* / *"Here's what nobody realizes."* Then deliver. (This is the "hook → re-hook → hook →
re-hook" rhythm — keep re-opening curiosity every few seconds through the body.)
