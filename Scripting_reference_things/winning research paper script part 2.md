## ✅ THE WINNING HOOK (shipped)
> **"Google Cloud and OpenAI are running behind this Indian guy — he just solved the biggest
> problem in training AI models."**

Why it works (steal this recipe):
- **Underdog vs. giants:** one named person (Subho Ghosh) beating Google/OpenAI. Instant
  root-for-the-hero.
- **Named villains = credibility + scale:** the giants make the stakes huge.
- **Big-stakes claim:** "the biggest problem in training AI models."
- **Simple words**, a real human at the center. (The concrete anchor — "Lighthouse Attention,
  21×" — lands in the body, which is fine because the hook already hooked.)

---

## 🧠 THE BODY — beat by beat
1. **Meet the hero + the finding tease:** an AI/ML researcher + 2 others made training *faster
   and cheaper* — "the finding is insane."
2. **The everyday stakes (plain):** ChatGPT/Claude were trained on millions of data points —
   "brutally expensive and slow."
3. **The one-line payoff number:** Lighthouse Attention = **21× faster, 2× cheaper**, without
   losing quality.
4. **Why the old way was a trap (the false choice):** before, you picked expensive-but-good OR
   cheap-but-bad. Everyone picked expensive.
5. **The method in 3 clean steps (this is the retention engine):**
   - **Compress** — squeeze the giant input into short summaries.
   - **Select** — keep only the few summaries that matter, throw the rest.
   - **Heal** — train cheap the whole way, then switch to full power at the very end to fine-tune.
6. **The knockout twist:** the *cheap*-trained model **beat** the expensive one on quality.
7. **Zoom out (trend tie-in):** last week AI could train itself; this shows how to do it 21×
   faster — "we're moving toward autonomous AI."
8. **CTA:** "Which AI lab adopts this first? Comment your bet. Follow for AI breakdowns before
   everyone catches on."

---

## 🎯 WHY IT WON (the transferable lessons)
- **A named human beating famous giants** is the page's most reliable hook engine. If the paper
  has a nameable author/lab/underdog, lead with them.
- **3 labeled steps** (Compress → Select → Heal) keep people watching — clear progress markers,
  one idea per beat.
- **Reveal the counterintuitive result late as a punch:** "the cheap one *beat* the expensive one."
- **Tie to a running trend** ("last week we saw X, this is the next step") — makes the page feel
  like a series people follow.

## ♻️ REUSABLE SKELETON
```
HOOK:  [Famous giants] are running behind [named underdog] — [he/they] just [big claim].
HERO:  Who they are + the insane finding, teased.
STAKES: The everyday expensive/slow/broken thing everyone accepts. (plain words)
NUMBER: The one payoff stat (×, %, or cheaper).
TRAP:  The false choice everyone was stuck in.
STEPS: The method in 3 labeled, one-idea steps.
PUNCH: The counterintuitive win (the "cheap/small one beat the big one").
TREND: Tie to last week / where this is all heading.
CTA:   A bet-style question + "follow before everyone catches on."
```
