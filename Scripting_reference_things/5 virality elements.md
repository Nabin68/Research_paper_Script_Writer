## PSYCHOLOGICAL TRIGGER INTEGRATION
 
### 1. Curiosity Triggers
- Create information gaps viewers need to close
- "But here's what most people don't know..."
- "Here's the hidden evil genius strategy..."
 
### 2. Status & Identity Triggers
- Position content as status-enhancing knowledge
- "This is what keeps Elon up at night."
- "I would call you a genius if you have heard of it before."

- "The next big natural cola brand could come from India."
- "This isn't science fiction anymore. It's happening right now."
 
### 4. Emotional Pattern Disruption
- Create cognitive dissonance
