## MASTER HOOK PRINCIPLES
 
### What Makes a Viral Hook:
1. **Pattern interrupt** - Stop the scroll immediately
2. **Under 15 words** - Quick, punchy delivery
3. **Specific numbers** - Credibility builders
4. **Challenge beliefs** - "What you thought was wrong"
5. **Promise value** - Clear benefit for watching
### Hook Styles That Work:
1. "But then." - Dance between context and conflict
2. **Zig zag sentences** - Varying sentence lengths
3. "The last dab." - Start with the ending
4. **Unique perspective** - Different angle on common topic
5. **Visual hook** - "Look at this..." / "You see this..."
