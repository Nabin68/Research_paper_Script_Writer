**In 2021, Google's AlphaFold changed that — an AI that could look at a protein and predict its shape almost instantly. It was such a leap it won a Nobel Prize.**
**But AlphaFold has a hard limit: to learn, it needed real, already-solved 3D shapes as training examples — something like 200,000 of them, like flashcards.**

**Researchers pulled that hidden knowledge out and used it to build a public atlas of 6.8 billion proteins, including 1.1 billion predicted 3D structures — and in head-to-head tests, it beat AlphaFold and every other leading structure-prediction AI.**
