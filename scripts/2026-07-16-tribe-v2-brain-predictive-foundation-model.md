Verified facts (checked against Meta AI's official blog post, 2026-07-16):
- TRIBE v2 is a tri-modal (video, audio, language) foundation model that predicts human brain
  activity — described by Meta as a "digital twin of human neural activity."
- Trained on a unified dataset of 1,000+ hours of fMRI brain scans from 700+ healthy volunteers
  watching videos, listening to podcasts, and reading text.
- Predicts whole-brain activity across ~70,000 points (voxels) — a 70x resolution increase over
  its predecessor, the award-winning Algonauts 2025 model, which only covered ~1,000 cortical
  points and was trained on just 4 people.
- Makes reliable zero-shot predictions for new subjects, new languages, and new tasks it was
  never trained on.
- Works by feeding pretrained video/audio/text embeddings into one shared transformer that learns
  patterns common to all three senses and to the brain itself.
- Meta released the model weights, codebase, paper, and an interactive demo (CC BY-NC) to speed
  up neuroscience research and disease treatment discovery.
