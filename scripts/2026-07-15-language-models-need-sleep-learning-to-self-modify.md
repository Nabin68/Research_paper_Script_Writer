Verified facts (web-checked, 2026-07-15):
- Paper: "Language Models Need Sleep: Learning to Self-Modify and Consolidate Memories" — arXiv
  2606.03979 (June 2026). Authors: Ali Behrouz, Farnoosh Hashemi, Adel Javanmard, Vahab Mirrokni.
- Same authors as Titans (2024, AI long-term memory) and Nested Learning/HOPE (Dec 2025) — this
  paper builds directly on the HOPE architecture. Real research lineage, not a one-off.
- Two-stage "Sleep": (1) Memory Consolidation/"Knowledge Seeding" — distills short-term
  in-context memories into permanent weights; (2) "Dreaming" — the model uses RL to generate its
  own synthetic practice data and rehearses on it, unsupervised.
- Results: beats EWC/InCA on continual learning (CLINC, Banking77, DBpedia); beats ICL/DuoAttention
  on long-context (LongHealth, QASPER, RULER); near-perfect recall scaling to 10 million tokens on
  BABILong, while rival methods (Titans, ARMT) degrade past 1 million; best on SQuAD knowledge
  incorporation; 80% success on ARC few-shot reasoning; beats standard SFT/GRPO training on math
  reasoning improvement.
- ⚠️ The paper explicitly calls itself a "proof of concept" — evaluated at modest/academic scale,
  not shown yet at frontier-model size. Keep this honest in the close.
