Verified facts (web-checked, 2026-07-15):
- SensorFM: a foundation model trained on 1+ trillion minutes (2+ billion hours) of wearable
  sensor data from 5 million consented people across 100+ countries and all 50 US states
  (Sept 2024 – Sept 2025), from Fitbit and Pixel Watch devices.
- Ingests 34 one-minute aggregate features from 5 sensors (PPG, accelerometer, EDA, skin
  temperature, altimeter) over a 24-hour window.
- Evaluated on 35 health/behavioral prediction tasks (cardiovascular, metabolic, sleep,
  mental-health, lifestyle, demographic) — outperformed specialized/bespoke models on 34 of 35.
- When powering a personal health AI agent, summaries using SensorFM's predictions were
  statistically indistinguishable from summaries using the person's actual known health data.
- ⚠️ Important caveat (from the researchers themselves): built for **screening/risk
  stratification, not clinical diagnosis** — no clinical deployment or independent validation yet,
  and some outcomes it was tested against are self-reported (questionnaires/medication records),
  not clinically confirmed. Keep this honest in the script — don't overclaim "diagnoses disease."
