**[BODY]**

**Ever wonder why your smartwatch tracks your heart, sleep, and stress all day — then does almost nothing with it? It just shows numbers on a screen.**

**Here's the real reason: every feature needed its own hand-built AI — heart, sleep, stress, each a separate model on its own tiny dataset. So your watch stayed a bunch of trackers that never understood you.**

**That's what Google set out to fix. They built one model instead — SensorFM — trained on over a trillion minutes of data from five million people in 100+ countries, learning how the body behaves.**

**And here's where it gets wild. This one model beat the specialized, hand-built tools on 34 of 35 health tasks. Then, powering a health assistant, doctors couldn't tell its read on your health from one built on your real data.**

**How? Instead of learning one disease at a time, it watched raw signals — a full day of heart, movement, temperature and sweat — and taught itself what a healthy body looks like. Then it can answer almost any health question.**

**The honest catch: this was tested in research, not a clinic — it flags risk, it doesn't diagnose. But if it holds up, your watch could catch a problem months before you'd feel it. We've worn devices that counted everything and understood nothing — Google built the part that finally understands.**
