Every AI company is panicking right now. A Korean lab just found a way that makes your AI agent 63% smarter and burn less tokens. Which means your AI bill is about to drop now. So here's the story. Researchers gave AI a Sudoku puzzle to solve across 7 levels with same difficulty. Just task length changed at each level. So after some success in level 1 and 2, the AI agent failed. Consistently. Think of it like running a race. You can sprint 100 meters, maybe 500. But you collapse when it comes to running a few kilometers. And the exact same way AI also works. And here are 3 reasons why AI breaks inside a long task. 1. Path explosion. So there are too many ways to solve a task but there is no feedback loop in the execution steps. And that's why the agent gets lost before it learns anything. 2. Blame confusion. Suppose a mistake happens on step 40 of a 60 step task. The model has no idea which step went wrong. So it rejects every step. Even the right ones. 3. Broken training. These failures poison the data and the model gets dumber with every long task. So the researchers tried something completely opposite and trained the models on shorter tasks instead of longer ones. And the results are insane. These models outperform the long trained ones by 63% on the exact same long task. Everyone is racing to build agents that run longer. But the bottleneck was never how long an agent can run. It was how short you train it to think. Follow for more AI breakdowns like this.
