## Caption

OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic have been training their AI models completely wrong, and it's inflating your AI bill.
Every AI model is built as a stack of layers.
Your question enters the bottom, moves up through every layer, and the answer comes out at the top.

After the model is built, the labs run reinforcement learning to sharpen it at math and reasoning.
Standard practice at every big lab: update every single layer at once.
Nobody questioned it.

Then a team at the University of Minnesota asked one dumb-sounding question.
What if you only trained ONE layer?

They tried it across seven models.
