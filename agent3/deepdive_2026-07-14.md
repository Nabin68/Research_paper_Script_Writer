## 4. 🟡 AI is teaching itself to get smarter — weak-to-strong distillation · **Wow 6/10**
**What it is:** "Weak-to-Strong Generalization via Direct On-Policy Distillation." Run cheap RL
on a **small** model, then transfer that learned policy shift to a **stronger** model — so a
weaker AI effectively trains a stronger one, skipping the most expensive step in building
frontier models.

**Wow factors:** "A weaker AI just taught a smarter one to reason — the student is training the
professor." · Frontier training gets radically cheaper.

**Internet virality — 2.5/4:** **#1 most-upvoted paper in the world today (81 HF upvotes)** and
a busy research direction ([curated OPD list](https://github.com/chrisliu298/awesome-on-policy-distillation),
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05394)) — but zero mainstream coverage; very inside-baseball.

**Channel fit — 3.5/6:** Great *reversal* framing, but the mechanism (RL rollouts, distillation)
is abstract and hard to make visceral in 60s — retention risk unless you fully humanize it

TurboQuant "saves Google billions" worked; raw efficiency doesn't).

**Why maybe:** Best raw popularity, but the topic is the type that needs the most scripting work
to not feel technical. Green-light only with a killer humanized hook.
