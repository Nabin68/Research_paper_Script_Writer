- Paper: "Language Models Need Sleep: Learning to Self-Modify and Consolidate Memories" — arXiv
  2606.03979 (June 2026). Authors: Ali Behrouz, Farnoosh Hashemi, Adel Javanmard, Vahab Mirrokni.
- Same authors as Titans (AI long-term memory) and Nested Learning/HOPE — this paper builds on the
  HOPE architecture. Real lineage, not a one-off.
- Two-stage "Sleep": (1) Memory Consolidation / "Knowledge Seeding" — distills short-term
  in-context memories into permanent weights; (2) "Dreaming" — the model uses RL to generate its
  own synthetic practice data and rehearses on it, unsupervised.
- Headline result: near-perfect recall scaling to 10 million tokens on BABILong, while rivals
  (Titans, ARMT) degrade past 1 million.
- ⚠️ The paper explicitly calls itself a "proof of concept" — modest/academic scale, not shown at
  frontier-model size. Kept honest in the close.
